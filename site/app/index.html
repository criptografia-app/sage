Try it with your wallet

Your own Safe, two AI co-signers, five minutes.

Connect a wallet on Sepolia. You get a 2-of-3 Safe owned by you and two demo agents. Propose a payment, watch the policy engine decide, and sign when it asks for you. Everything happens on the Sepolia test network, and your wallet pays the gas (about 0.003 test ETH).

  1. Connect your walletMetaMask or any browser wallet, on Sepolia.
  2. Create your SafeOwners: you, proposal agent …, signer agent …. Any 2 of 3.
  3. Fund itSend it a little test ETH to play with.
    ETH
  4. Ask the agents to pay someone
